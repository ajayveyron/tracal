Architecture Delivery Validation Changelog

iPhone · paid once · local first

Public without becoming invasive.

Tracal is a $19.99 paid-once iPhone app with no account, backend, remote database, ads, or analytics. The first 100 buyers pay an $8.99 launch price. People bring one provider API key; meals and usage history remain on their device.

$19.99Standard US price; $8.99 for the first 100 buyers
No Tracal loginNo Sign in with Apple because there is no account
Direct BYOKOpenAI, Anthropic, or Gemini—never a router
Device-ownedSwiftData, protected images, local usage history

Launch pricing: publish at $8.99, monitor completed purchases in App Store Connect, and change the US base price to $19.99 as soon as the recorded count reaches 100. The offer is a one-time purchase, not a lifetime subscription. Because App Store pricing cannot switch on buyer count automatically, record the count and change timestamp; reporting delay may allow a small overrun.

One app. No service layer.

The app performs deterministic parsing and Vision OCR first. New cloud inference goes only to the provider selected in Settings. Apple Intelligence remains the offline text fallback.

Data boundary: provider keys use WhenUnlockedThisDeviceOnly Keychain storage. They never enter SwiftData, UserDefaults, backups, logs, crash metadata, or the app bundle. Network sessions are ephemeral, official API origins are hardcoded, and there is no cross-provider fallback.

Release sequence.

Manual release keeps the launch reversible. Public promotion begins only after the production binary has been quiet for one day.

Finish automated coverage

Provider fixtures, pricing, retry accounting, backup/import, deletion, and StoreKit scenarios.

Internal TestFlight

10–20 people validate setup clarity, key safety, meal logging, and crash telemetry from Apple.

External TestFlight

Recruit 50–100 iPhone users from LinkedIn. Require at least 30 to use the app for seven days.

Submit manually

Use sample-meal review mode, direct-provider disclosure, the deployed Tracal support URLs, and manual release.

Quiet production day

Release without announcement, inspect crashes, reviews, refunds, and provider failures for 24 hours.

Announce and monitor

Publish LinkedIn and beta-list email on day two. Continue focused monitoring for 14 days.

The release gate.

A compiling app is not a public-ready app. Every item below must be evidenced on the release candidate or explicitly waived with a dated reason.

No credential, meal body, or sensitive header appears in source, logs, diagnostics, or crash metadata.

Network inspection shows provider requests reaching only official OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google API origins.

Every default model passes at least 20 text and 20 photo fixtures with strict schema validation.

Provider token totals match native responses; retries are separate; missing usage remains unknown.

Estimated cost applies the matching provider, model, catalog version, and effective date.

OCR, deterministic text, Apple Intelligence, history, edit, export, import, and deletion work without API keys.

Revocation, repurchase, refund link, and offline StoreKit behavior pass without false lockout.

Camera and Photos flows do not disturb the system Camera app on physical devices.

VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, Reduce Motion, dark mode, and keyboard flows pass.

External TestFlight reaches at least 99.5% crash-free sessions before submission.
