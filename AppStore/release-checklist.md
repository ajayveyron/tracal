# Tracal release checklist

## Account and commercial

- [ ] Accept the current Paid Apps Agreement.
- [ ] Complete tax and banking verification.
- [ ] Confirm individual seller name.
- [ ] Set US base price to $8.99.
- [ ] Exclude EU storefronts for v1.
- [ ] Enroll in the Small Business Program if eligible.

## App information

- [ ] Reserve the Tracal name and confirm trademark clearance.
- [ ] Upload app icon and five iPhone screenshots.
- [ ] Set Health & Fitness and Food & Drink categories.
- [ ] Complete the 13+ age-rating questionnaire.
- [ ] Declare that Tracal is not a regulated medical device.
- [ ] Complete export-compliance questions.
- [ ] Publish privacy, terms, and support URLs.
- [ ] Complete the privacy nutrition label for health and user content sent to providers.

## Validation

- [ ] Verify no keys or request bodies appear in logs or crash metadata.
- [ ] Run text and photo fixtures for every default model.
- [ ] Verify provider-reported tokens and cost estimates.
- [ ] Verify backup/import and full deletion.
- [ ] Verify paid-app revocation and offline StoreKit behavior.
- [ ] Verify Camera and Photos on physical devices without Device Hub screen sharing.
- [ ] Complete VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, Reduce Motion, dark mode, and keyboard passes.
- [ ] Reach 99.5% crash-free TestFlight sessions before release.

## Launch

- [ ] Internal TestFlight: 10–20 people.
- [ ] External TestFlight: 50–100 people.
- [ ] At least 30 people use Tracal for seven days.
- [ ] Submit with manual release.
- [ ] Quiet release for 24 hours.
- [ ] LinkedIn announcement and beta email on day two.
- [ ] Monitor crashes, reviews, refunds, provider changes, and support for 14 days.
