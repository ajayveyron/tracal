Apple handles app refunds.

Request a refund

App Store purchases and refund eligibility are controlled by Apple. Submit a request through Report a Problem.

Provider charges

Tracal cannot refund charges made by OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google. Contact the relevant provider about its API invoice or account balance.

Before deleting

Export a local backup if you want to preserve meals, goals, photos, and usage history. Provider keys are never included in that backup.
