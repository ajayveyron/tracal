Privacy, kept simple.

Stored on your iPhone

Meals, goals, protected photos, AI usage history, provider selection, and backups stay on your device. Tracal does not operate an account system, database, analytics service, or inference server.

Cloud analysis

When you explicitly connect OpenAI, Anthropic, or Gemini and allow cloud analysis, Tracal sends only the current meal description and sanitized photo to that provider. Requests go directly to the provider selected in Settings and are governed by that provider’s terms and privacy policy.

API keys

Provider keys use device-only Keychain protection. They are excluded from SwiftData, backups, logs, crash metadata, and the app bundle. A compromised device may still expose a key, so use a dedicated restricted key with a spending limit.

Deletion

Delete all Tracal data in Settings to remove meals, goals, photos, local usage history, preferences, and provider keys. Deleting the app removes its sandbox; Tracal also clears stale Keychain credentials when it detects a fresh installation.
